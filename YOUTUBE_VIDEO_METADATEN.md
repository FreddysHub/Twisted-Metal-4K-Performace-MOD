## Beschreibung (EN)

Fan-made rendering and performance project for Twisted Metal (PS3 / BCUS98106 update 01.06) on a modified RPCS3 build.

Source and build guide:
https://github.com/FreddysHub/Twisted-Metal-4K-Performace-MOD

Reference target: 3840 × 2160 output and up to 60 FPS. Actual performance depends on hardware, scene, build and cache state. This is not a universal 60 FPS or zero-latency benchmark, a replacement texture pack, or an official Sony/RPCS3 release. Use your own matching game and firmware.

Development: custom Vulkan scene/colour reconstruction, selective PostFX bypass, community FPS/MLAA patches, frame/crop alignment and targeted distant-detail asset changes.

FreddysHub directed and repeatedly tested the project, with AI-assisted analysis/code from Google Gemini / Antigravity and OpenAI ChatGPT / Codex. Thanks to RPCS3 contributors, community patch authors including illusion and Whatcookie, and RenderDoc.
